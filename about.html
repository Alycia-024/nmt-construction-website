WHO WE ARE

A Company Built Around Getting the Work Done Properly.

NMT Construction and Engineering (Pty) Ltd was established on 07 July 2020 with a focus on providing dependable construction and engineering services to clients with different project needs.

We understand that successful projects require more than simply completing a task. Good planning, communication, workmanship and attention to detail all play an important role in achieving the required result.

Our approach is practical and project-focused. We take the time to understand what is required, consider the conditions of the work and apply solutions that are appropriate to the project.
