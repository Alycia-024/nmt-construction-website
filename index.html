ABOUT NMT

Built on Work That Speaks for Itself.

NMT Construction and Engineering (Pty) Ltd was established on 07 July 2020 with a clear focus: to provide dependable construction and engineering services that get the job done properly.

From groundwork and building to maintenance and engineering solutions, we approach each project with attention to detail, practical planning and a strong commitment to the finished result.

We understand that no two projects are the same. Our approach is straightforward — understand what is required, plan the work, communicate clearly and deliver to the required standard.
